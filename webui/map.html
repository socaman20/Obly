Your run list is kept here, not in the game. Star Citizen drops your route when you log out and keeps only your reputation, so a three-drop delivery lives in your head. Queue the stops here and you only ever plot the next leg in game \u2014 press N or Next stop. Finished runs are logged with the date.

Source. Systems, jump tunnels and all 866 celestial objects come from robertsspaceindustries.com/api/starmap — CIG's own service. Galaxy positions are real galactic coordinates. Inside a system, bodies are placed at their real orbital distance on a logarithmic scale (Stanton's moons sit at 0.0006 AU while its jump points sit at 4.85, so a linear scale would put everything on top of the star); the angle around the orbit is stable per body but not the live in-game position, which CIG does not publish. Route paths follow the real jump network.
